## Event title (use as-is)

**I Code With My Voice — Live Hands-Free Coding with Trillium Smith**

## Blurb (paste to Meetup / Eventbrite / newsletter)

> Trillium Smith is a software developer and open-source contributor who codes hands-free using tools like Talon Voice and Cursorless. He builds developer and AI tooling and enjoys sharing what he's learned about alternative ways of interacting with computers. Outside of code, he's a former massage therapist and salsa dance instructor.
>
> In this live demo, Trillium codes hands-free in front of your eyes — no keyboard, no mouse — and breaks down how voice-driven development actually works. You'll leave with a practical feel for accessibility-first tooling and ideas you can steal for your own workflow, whether or not you ever ditch your keyboard. No prior voice-coding experience needed. Q&A included.

## What the org provides

- Large screen / projector + HDMI, readable from back row
- Wi-Fi + power outlet, 1 chair + small table
- Quiet-ish room (voice recognition likes low background noise)
- Mic for audience questions; captions on if available
- Virtual: screen-share + computer audio, captions on

Trillium brings: laptop, wireless mic, full voice-coding setup, demo project.

Voice-break note: sessions over 60 min get one 5-min voice break mid-way.
