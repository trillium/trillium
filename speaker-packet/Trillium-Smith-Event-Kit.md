## Short bio

> Trillium Smith is a software developer and open-source contributor who codes hands-free using tools like Talon Voice and Cursorless. He builds developer and AI tooling and enjoys sharing what he's learned about alternative ways of interacting with computers. Outside of code, he's a former massage therapist and salsa dance instructor.

## Presentation

**I Code With My Voice — Here's How**

> In this live demo, Trillium Smith codes hands-free in front of your eyes — no keyboard, no mouse — and breaks down how voice-driven development actually works. Using Talon Voice and Cursorless, he'll write real code live, narrate every step in plain language, and show how AI agents fit into a hands-free workflow. You'll leave with a practical feel for accessibility-first tooling and ideas you can steal for your own setup, whether or not you ever ditch your keyboard. No prior voice-coding experience needed. Q&A included.
