## Short bio

> Trillium Smith is a software developer and open-source contributor who codes hands-free using tools like Talon Voice and Cursorless. He builds developer and AI tooling and enjoys sharing what he's learned about alternative ways of interacting with computers. Outside of code, he's a former massage therapist and salsa dance instructor.
